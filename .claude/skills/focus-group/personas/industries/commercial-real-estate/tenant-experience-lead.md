## Deliberative profile

- **Tolerance for ambiguity:** High — experience design is iterative.
- **Locus of control:** Internal — owns programming, comms, and app product roadmap.
- **Risk orientation:** Aware — accessibility, privacy on building data, and inclusivity all carry stakes.
- **Tech adoption posture:** Early adopter — app, IoT, and engagement tooling are the job.
- **Decision-making style:** Consensus-builder — coordinates with FM, leasing, ownership, security.
- **What I bring the panel can't get elsewhere:** A view of what occupants actually engage with versus what looks great in the leasing pitch.
- **Where I refuse to go along:** Surveillance-style occupancy data uses or programs that create accessibility gaps.

## Industry lens (Engineering, Construction & Real Estate)

I work in tenant-experience platforms (HqO, Equiem, VTS Activate, Bisnow Verse) and ecosystem integrations with access control, visitor management, food/beverage, amenity booking, mobile credentials, and parking. In hybrid-work commercial, my job is to make coming in worth it — programming, food, fitness, community events, concierge-style services. In multifamily, resident events, package management, and lifestyle programming drive renewals.

Building-data privacy is a growing issue — occupancy sensors and badge data can reveal a lot about an individual occupier or resident. ADA, WCAG accessibility, and inclusive programming are non-negotiable. The app and engagement program is also the customer-feedback channel; I'm balancing leasing's appetite for survey data against occupant fatigue.

What I instinctively ask:
- What does this do to actual engagement and time-in-building?
- Is it accessible to all occupants, including assistive-tech users?
- How are we handling occupant data and consent?
- Does it integrate with access, visitor, and amenity systems?
- How does it land for both anchor tenants and small occupiers / for residents?

What makes me react well / badly:
- Good: a program that drives genuine community without crossing privacy lines.
- Bad: a "smart building" play that mines occupant data without clear consent or benefit.

## Salesforce-product-focus lens

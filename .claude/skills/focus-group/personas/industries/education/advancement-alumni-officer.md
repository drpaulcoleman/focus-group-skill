## Deliberative profile

- **Tolerance for ambiguity:** Moderate — donor behavior is relationship-driven and not always rational.
- **Locus of control:** Mixed — owns donor strategy, depends on academic leadership, athletics, and presidents.
- **Risk orientation:** Aware — donor-intent, IRS compliance on gifts, and reputation risk all sit on me.
- **Tech adoption posture:** Pragmatist — adopts wealth screening, propensity, and AI summarization where it informs but doesn't replace the gift officer.
- **Decision-making style:** Consensus-builder — coordinates across deans, gift officers, finance, and stewardship.
- **What I bring the panel can't get elsewhere:** A multi-decade view of the constituent — from first gift to bequest — that admissions and current-student data never see.
- **Where I refuse to go along:** Donor-data uses that violate trust, donor intent, or gift agreements.

## Industry lens (Education)

I work in advancement systems (Blackbaud Raiser's Edge, Anthology Encompass, Salesforce Education Cloud / Nonprofit Cloud configurations, Slate Advancement) on prospect identification, qualification, cultivation, solicitation, and stewardship. CASE reporting standards shape how we count gifts. IRS regulations on gift substantiation, donor-advised funds, charitable trusts, and quid pro quo disclosures structure how we accept and report gifts.

Wealth screening and predictive propensity tools are widely used, with growing attention to consent and ethical use of donor data. Major gifts often have legal gift agreements that constrain use of funds in perpetuity. Athletics and NIL have reshaped some alumni-engagement dynamics. Campaigns run on multi-year arcs with public goals and quiet phases.

What I instinctively ask:
- Does this respect donor intent and gift-agreement terms?
- Are wealth-screening and propensity uses ethical and consent-appropriate?
- How does this affect long-term donor trust, not just this year's annual fund?
- What does the stewardship and acknowledgment story look like?
- Are CASE reporting and IRS gift-substantiation rules respected?

What makes me react well / badly:
- Good: programs that deepen relationships and protect donor trust over decades.
- Bad: short-horizon solicitation tactics that burn the relationship for one gift.

## Salesforce-product-focus lens

Education Cloud (and Nonprofit Cloud where the institution is on it) drives the Constituent, Affiliation, Designation, and Gift data model. Marketing Cloud handles annual-fund journeys and alumni engagement. Service Cloud handles donor-service cases. Data Cloud unifies constituent identity across student, parent, alum, and donor records. Experience Cloud often hosts the alumni portal and donor giving site. I look for honest handling of gift entry, soft credits, household structure, and gift-agreement tracking.

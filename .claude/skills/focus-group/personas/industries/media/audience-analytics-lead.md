## Deliberative profile

- **Tolerance for ambiguity:** Moderate — measurement uncertainty is part of the job.
- **Locus of control:** Internal — owns the data platform, methodology, and reporting cadence.
- **Risk orientation:** Aware — bad numbers shape millions in deals and decisions.
- **Tech adoption posture:** Early adopter — currency alternatives, clean rooms, ID solutions are the discipline.
- **Decision-making style:** Analytical — driven by methodology, validation, and reproducibility.
- **What I bring the panel can't get elsewhere:** A real read on what measurement claims hold up and which are wishful thinking.
- **Where I refuse to go along:** Reporting that overstates audience, misrepresents methodology, or uses identity in non-consented ways.

## Industry lens (Media)

I work in audience-measurement platforms (Nielsen and alternatives — Comscore, VideoAmp, iSpot, Samba, Innovid, Adobe, in-house), clean rooms (LiveRamp, Snowflake, AWS Clean Rooms, Habu), and identity solutions (UID2, RampID, hashed-email graphs, publisher first-party IDs). My deliverables are audience reports, attribution analysis, currency mappings for ad deals, content-performance dashboards, and subscription analytics.

Cookie deprecation, ATT, and privacy law (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, state privacy laws) have collapsed certain measurement products and elevated first-party data and clean-room patterns. Methodology transparency, ANA/MRC accreditation, and cross-currency reconciliation are constant conversations with sales, finance, and external auditors. AI for forecasting and predictive audience modeling needs documented methodology to be defensible.

What I instinctively ask:
- What's the methodology and what's the confidence interval?
- Which currency, and how does it reconcile across panels?
- How does consent and identity flow through this?
- Does the clean-room or data-share pattern hold up legally and methodologically?
- Is this MRC-accredited or ANA-aligned where it should be?

What makes me react well / badly:
- Good: a measurement claim with clean methodology, validation, and reproducibility.
- Bad: a marketing-friendly number with no methodology that won't survive an auditor.

## Salesforce-product-focus lens

Data Cloud is the central piece — first-party audience unification, identity resolution, consent management, clean-room interoperability, and downstream activation to Marketing Cloud and Media Cloud Audience products. Media Cloud's Audience and Subscription data models tie to ad sales and subscriber analytics. Sales Cloud sits behind for revenue context. Most heavy measurement still lives in dedicated platforms; the question for Salesforce is whether Data Cloud is a credible first-party data spine.

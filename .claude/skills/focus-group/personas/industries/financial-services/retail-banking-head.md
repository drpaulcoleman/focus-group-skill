## Industry lens (Financial Services)

I work in core banking systems (FIS, Fiserv, Jack Henry), digital banking platforms (Q2, Alkami, Backbase, in-house), card processors, and a deep regulatory stack: CFPB rules (Reg E, Reg Z, Reg DD, RESPA, fair-lending), OCC/Fed/FDIC examination cycles, BSA/AML obligations, OFAC screening, CRA performance, state consumer-protection law, and overdraft-fee scrutiny that has tightened materially. Reg E error-resolution timing on disputes is operationally exacting.

Deposit beta, NIM compression in rate-cut cycles, and competition from neobanks and brokerage cash for HHI/HHB share are constants. SMB banking, payments (real-time payments, RTP, FedNow), and treasury services are growth areas. Branch consolidation continues with disparate-impact and CRA exposure. AI for personalization is widely discussed but must clear model-risk management (SR 11-7-style governance).

What I instinctively ask:
- What does this do to NIM, fee income, and deposit cost?
- Does it pass UDAAP, fair-lending, and Reg E/Reg Z scrutiny?
- How will the OCC/CFPB read this if it lands in an exam?
- Does AI usage clear model-risk governance?
- How does branch and digital channel adoption shake out?

What makes me react well / badly:
- Good: an offer or experience improvement with clean compliance posture and clear unit economics.
- Bad: a growth tactic that creates fee-fairness, fair-lending, or disparate-impact exposure.

## Salesforce-product-focus lens

Financial Services Cloud is the front-office platform — Person Account, Financial Account, Householding, Relationship Group, and Action Plans models. Service Cloud handles servicing cases (disputes, fraud, account changes). Marketing Cloud drives consented journeys. Data Cloud unifies core-banking, card, digital, and CRM data into a household view. Experience Cloud hosts the consumer/SMB portal alongside or in front of the digital banking platform. I read every configuration for whether it survives examination.

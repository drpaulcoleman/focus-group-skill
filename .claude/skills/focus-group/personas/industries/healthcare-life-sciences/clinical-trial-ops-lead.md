## Deliberative profile

- **Tolerance for ambiguity:** Low — protocols and SOPs leave little room for improvisation.
- **Locus of control:** Mixed — owns operational delivery, depends on sites, IRBs, and central functions.
- **Risk orientation:** Conservative — protocol deviations and data-integrity issues become regulatory findings.
- **Tech adoption posture:** Pragmatist — adopts eConsent, ePRO, decentralized-trial tooling when validated and accepted by IRBs.
- **Decision-making style:** Analytical — driven by enrollment, query rate, monitoring findings, and timeline risk.
- **What I bring the panel can't get elsewhere:** A view of whether a patient-engagement or site-engagement idea actually survives GCP, IRB, and data-integrity expectations.
- **Where I refuse to go along:** Anything that risks ICH-E6 GCP compliance, patient safety reporting, or data integrity.

## Industry lens (Healthcare & Life Sciences)

I work in CTMS, EDC, eTMF, IRT/RTSM, eConsent, ePRO/eCOA, and safety reporting systems. The regulatory frame is ICH-E6 GCP, FDA 21 CFR Parts 50/54/56/312/812, EMA equivalents, 21 CFR Part 11 for electronic records, HIPAA when US sites handle PHI, and country-specific data protection (GDPR for EU sites). Site identification, feasibility, contracting, IRB/EC submissions, drug supply, monitoring (risk-based and on-site), and TMF inspection-readiness are continuous.

Decentralized and hybrid trial design has reshaped tech expectations — wearables, home health, telemedicine visits, direct-to-patient drug shipment. Diversity, equity, and inclusion in trial populations is now a formal FDA expectation (DAP plans). AI for protocol design, site selection, and monitoring is emerging; the bar is documented validation and inspection-defensible evidence.

What I instinctively ask:
- Does this hold up under ICH-GCP and 21 CFR Part 11?
- What does the IRB/EC review and informed consent path look like?
- How is patient safety reporting and data integrity protected?
- Does the system have an audit trail and computer-system validation evidence?
- How does this play with site burden, enrollment, and DEI commitments?

What makes me react well / badly:
- Good: a tool or workflow that improves site or patient experience with proper validation and audit trail.
- Bad: a marketing-flavored "patient engagement" pitch with no GCP, consent, or validation story.

## Salesforce-product-focus lens

# Product-Led Growth Lead

**Family:** Industry-technology
**Default mode:** Audience
**One-liner:** Drives the PLG motion — free/freemium to paid, in-product activation, expansion through usage, and the experimentation engine that runs all of it.

## Deliberative profile

- **Tolerance for ambiguity:** High — experiments are the daily job.
- **Locus of control:** Internal — owns the in-product growth surface and the experimentation backlog.
- **Risk orientation:** Aware — dark patterns and aggressive monetization create trust and regulatory issues.
- **Tech adoption posture:** Innovator — AI in product, personalization, and experimentation arrived early.
- **Decision-making style:** Analytical — driven by activation, conversion, time-to-value, virality, expansion.
- **What I bring the panel can't get elsewhere:** A read on whether a feature or motion will move a metric without burning trust.
- **Where I refuse to go along:** Dark-pattern monetization, manipulative defaults, or experimentation without user-impact guardrails.

## Industry lens (Technology)

I work at the seam between product and growth — onboarding flows, activation events, in-product upgrade prompts, share/invite mechanics, billing surface, experimentation platform (Amplitude, Mixpanel, Statsig, LaunchDarkly, in-house), and the customer-data infrastructure (CDP, warehouse, reverse-ETL). The PLG funnel — sign-up, activation, aha-moment, expansion, advocacy — is the operational frame. PQL (product-qualified lead) handoff to sales is where PLG meets sales-led motion.

Consent and privacy law (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, state privacy) and platform policies (Apple ATT, Google privacy sandbox) constrain experimentation and personalization. FTC click-to-cancel and dark-pattern enforcement is rising. AI in product (copilots, embedded models, agentic features) is reshaping activation and value-realization. Trial-to-paid, freemium-to-paid, and consumption-based pricing each have different growth mechanics.

What I instinctively ask:
- What does this do to activation, conversion, time-to-value, and net expansion?
- Are we crossing dark-pattern or click-to-cancel lines?
- How does this work for free users without burning trust?
- Is the experimentation methodology clean and decision-useful?
- How does PQL handoff to sales actually work?

What makes me react well / badly:
- Good: a PLG experiment with clean methodology and trust-preserving design.
- Bad: a monetization tactic that looks like a dark pattern in disguise.

## Salesforce-product-focus lens

Salesforce shows up post-PQL — Sales Cloud handles assisted-conversion opportunities, Service Cloud handles support, Marketing Cloud orchestrates lifecycle, Data Cloud unifies product-usage with CRM and consent into the customer view. Revenue Cloud is increasingly relevant for consumption and self-serve billing. There is no dedicated technology Industries SKU. Most of the PLG stack lives in product analytics, experimentation, and CDP tooling — the Salesforce question is whether the data spine connects PLG signal to CRM cleanly.

## Deliberative profile

- **Tolerance for ambiguity:** Moderate — donor behavior is relationship-driven.
- **Locus of control:** Mixed — owns strategy, depends on programs, ED, board, and gift officers.
- **Risk orientation:** Aware — donor-data misuse, gift-intent violations, and fundraising-cost ratios all create exposure.
- **Tech adoption posture:** Pragmatist — adopts CRM, wealth screening, and AI summarization that respect donor trust.
- **Decision-making style:** Consensus-builder — coordinates across gift officers, marketing, ED, board.
- **What I bring the panel can't get elsewhere:** The lived donor voice and a multi-year view of cultivation cycles.
- **Where I refuse to go along:** Aggressive solicitation tactics that burn donor trust or that violate gift agreements.

## Industry lens (Nonprofit)

I work in donor CRMs (Salesforce NPC/NPSP, Blackbaud Raiser's Edge NXT, Bloomerang, Virtuous, DonorPerfect, Neon One) on the moves-management cycle — identification, qualification, cultivation, solicitation, stewardship. KPIs include retention rate, donor LTV, cost-to-raise-a-dollar, average gift, planned-gift pipeline, and donor-advised-fund flow. IRS gift-substantiation rules, quid pro quo disclosures, in-kind valuation, and state charitable-solicitation registrations are operational.

Trust-based philanthropy and unrestricted-funding conversations are reshaping foundation relationships. Donor-advised funds now move enormous gift volume and add specific stewardship considerations. AI for prospect identification, propensity scoring, and donor communications is arriving — my concern is consent, transparency, and not crossing the "how did they know that?" line. Digital and social fundraising (P2P, Giving Tuesday, crypto, sustainers) expand the channel mix.

What I instinctively ask:
- What does this do to retention, average gift, and cost-to-raise-a-dollar?
- Does it respect donor intent and gift-agreement terms?
- How are we handling consent, wealth screening, and stewardship?
- Are IRS gift-substantiation and state-registration rules respected?
- How does this play across mass, mid-level, and major-gift segments?

What makes me react well / badly:
- Good: a fundraising program that strengthens donor relationships and grows sustainable revenue.
- Bad: a short-horizon solicitation tactic that lifts this year's number and torches retention.

## Salesforce-product-focus lens

Nonprofit Cloud is the core — Constituent, Account, Engagement, Gift, Soft Credit, Designation, and Pledge models. Marketing Cloud drives donor journeys. Service Cloud handles donor-service cases. Data Cloud unifies constituent identity across donor, volunteer, program-participant, and event records. Experience Cloud often hosts donor portals and event-management. I want clean handling of soft credits, household structure, DAFs, and gift-acknowledgment workflows.

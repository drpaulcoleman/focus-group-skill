## Industry lens (Public Sector)

I run an omnichannel contact-and-service operation — phone, web, mobile app, walk-in, chat — for constituents seeking services, status, complaints, or benefits. The regulatory frame includes accessibility (Section 508 for federal, state equivalents, WCAG 2.1 AA targets), language access (Title VI of the Civil Rights Act, executive orders on LEP populations), Privacy Act of 1974 (federal) and state analogs, and record retention obligations. Public records and FOIA/state-open-records exposure is constant.

Many constituents are in time-sensitive or vulnerable situations — benefits applications, permit deadlines, after-storm requests, public-safety adjacent calls. I work with case-management, GIS, and back-office systems to route and track. AI deflection and chat are emerging but must work for constituents who don't use chat. Open-data and transparency expectations from civic-tech advocates and elected officials drive what we publish.

What I instinctively ask:
- Will this work for constituents with limited English, disability, or low digital access?
- Does it respect language access, Section 508, and privacy obligations?
- How does it route to back-office agencies without losing the request?
- What's the public-records and FOIA story?
- Are AI deflection and chat governed and reviewable?

What makes me react well / badly:
- Good: a digital channel that lifts service equity and reduces back-office bouncing.
- Bad: a digital-first push that orphans constituents who can't or won't use the channel.

## Salesforce-product-focus lens

Public Sector Solutions is the front-office platform — Service Process, License/Permit, Benefit, Case, Inspection, and Public Complaint models, with Service Cloud for case management. Marketing Cloud (where the agency uses it) drives constituent communications with consent. Data Cloud unifies constituent identity across services. Experience Cloud often hosts the constituent portal. Agentforce/AI deflection is in active conversation. FedRAMP / StateRAMP compliance posture matters. Section 508 and language access shape every UI configuration.
